Who We Are

Building Relationships, Brick by Brick

Established in 2006, Nisha Group is one of the renowned real estate companies in Aurangabad. We have Business Interest in the field of Real Estate Development, Land Dealing, Property Dealing and Plotting.

Our focus has been on the development of premium residential and commercial projects in and around the city. We have undertaken & successfully completed projects ranging from high-end group housing projects to commercial & retail related buildings.

We are also focusing on affordable housing and sustainable development projects. Our commitment to quality & innovation have earned us the enduring trust of our customers and made us the renowned group - "Nisha Group".

  • Renowned real estate developer since 2006
  • Premium residential & commercial developments
  • Affordable housing & sustainable projects
  • 100% transparent & ethical business practices
  • MahaRERA compliant across all projects
